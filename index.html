Service Policy

Retouching Policy

I focus on enhancing your natural beauty through professional color grading and skin texture refinement. Notice: To preserve your authentic self, I do not offer structural alterations (such as body slimming or facial reshaping).

I do not take references from other photographers' work. Every photographer has their own unique shooting and editing style. If you are looking for a style that is different from my portfolio, I would kindly recommend finding a photographer whose work more closely matches what you have in mind.

Edited photos will be delivered via an online gallery within up to 1 month after the client selects the images for editing. All original and edited files will be sent via gallery links. Please note that the original gallery will be deleted after 2 weeks and the edited gallery after 1 week. Deleted files cannot be recovered, so we highly recommend downloading them in advance.

Usage Rights

The photographer holds the copyright to the images, and the client holds the portrait rights. The photographer will never use the photos for personal or promotional purposes without the client's consent.

Rescheduling & Cancellation

  • A 100% refund of the deposit is available if the booking is canceled within 3 days of making the reservation. Cancellations made after this period are strictly non-refundable.
  • Rescheduling is allowed once at no additional cost, provided the requested new date does not conflict with the photographer's existing schedule. Any further rescheduling after the first change will incur an additional fee.
  • Shoots will proceed as scheduled in light rain. In the event of heavy rain, natural disasters, or extreme weather conditions that make shooting impossible, the client may choose to either receive a refund or reschedule the shoot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How far in advance do I need to book?

Any date that is open on the booking calendar can be booked, up to the day before your shoot (same-day bookings are not available). Popular dates, especially weekends, fill up quickly, so booking early is recommended.

When is my booking confirmed?

Your date is secured only once the 50% deposit is confirmed. Submitting the booking form alone does not hold the date — if another client's deposit clears first, the date may no longer be available.

When will I receive my photos?

Edited photos are delivered via an online gallery within up to 1 month after you select the images for editing. The original gallery is deleted after 2 weeks and the edited gallery after 1 week. Deleted files cannot be recovered, so please download them in advance.

What if it rains on the day?

Shoots proceed as scheduled in light rain. In heavy rain, natural disasters, or extreme weather that makes shooting impossible, you may choose either a refund or a reschedule.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

A 100% deposit refund is available if you cancel within 3 days of booking; after that, deposits are non-refundable. Rescheduling is free once (subject to availability), and further changes incur an additional fee.

Can you slim my face or body in retouching?

No. Retouching covers color grading, brightness, skin blemishes, and hair — but not structural alterations such as body slimming or facial reshaping, to preserve your authentic self.
